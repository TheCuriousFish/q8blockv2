Step 2 of the `design` skill flow. Design system is locked in `clients/q8block/DESIGN.md` (NP Digital
ported exactly). NAP comes from `clients/q8block/company.md` and fills slots only.
Media decision, already made by Ahmad: **images only, no film.** There is no timeline film section.

**Revision, 2026-09-24.** This brief was rewritten after Ahmad corrected the page structure. The first
version built the whole homepage around the free promotion and sold to a named country. Both are now

## 1. Design read

**Who is buying.** The owner or manager of a local service business. Plumbing, AC, cleaning, pest
control, moving, maintenance, car wash. He already has a Google Business Profile, he already gets some
calls from the map, and he has no website. He is not shopping for `SEO`. He does not know what a
canonical tag is and does not want to. He knows one thing: people search for his service and sometimes
they call someone else.

**He is not defined by a country.** Ahmad, 2026-09-24: `anyone with a local service we can service,
whether they're in US, UK, UAE, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia or anywhere.` The buyer is defined by his situation,
not his passport, and the page says so by never naming a market.

**What he is afraid of.** Being sold a monthly retainer with nothing to show for it. Paying for a website
that sits there. Handing over his Google profile to a stranger. Being told a timeline that does not
happen.

**Register.** NP Digital's: confident, plain, outcome led, short sentences, no adjectives doing the work
of facts. Arabic in formal Modern Standard Arabic, matching how the ranking Arabic agency pages actually
write (Section 9). Not Gulf colloquial. Not the flowery `نفتخر بأن نكون شريكك الرقمي` register.

**What Q8Block cannot claim.** NP's argument is scale: offices, headcount, award walls. Q8Block is one
person, no team page, no testimonials collected yet, and Ahmad will not put his face on the site. So the
page never argues from size. Every persuasive beat comes from one of exactly two sources:

1. **The category correction.** Most agencies optimise the site you already have. Q8Block designs and
   builds the site, then gets it found in Google search and in AI answers, and keeps it running. That is a
   position, and it is free of both geography and promotion. The Google profile stays the client's and is
   handed back as a checklist rather than taken over, but that is an answer to a question, not a selling
   point: Ahmad cut it from Section 4 on 2026-09-24 as `weird to say`, and it lives in FAQ Q3 only.
2. **Real Search Console exports from real client sites.** Not claims about results, real exports, one
   named client and one named month at a time, reproduced from
   `clients/q8block/design/proof-data.md` and confined to two sections.

## 1b. The correction, 2026-09-24. Both parts are absolute.

**Q8Block is an SEO agency for local service businesses. We are building the brand, not the offer.**

**No geography in the brand copy.** Every mention of Saudi Arabia, the Gulf, Kuwait as a market, or any
country is out of the homepage, including the page title and the meta description. The audience phrase is
`شركات الخدمات المحلية` / `local service businesses`, never a nationality. The company's own address and
phone stay in the footer because that is NAP from `company.md`, not positioning. The registered name
`شركة كويت بلوك` survives only in the footer legal line.

**The offer gets two places and no more.** The thin strip at Section 2 (label pill, one line, countdown
slot, spots slot, one link) and its own page at `/offer`. Everything else that was offer led on the old
homepage, the big offer section, the eligibility block, the four offer FAQs, has moved to `/offer`. The
homepage sells the company; the offer page sells the promotion to a reader who has asked for it.

**This also resolved the old open item.** The previous brief flagged that every proof site is Kuwaiti
while the page sold to Saudi companies. With the homepage no longer stating who it sells to by location,
that tension is gone: the proof is simply proof, from named sites in named months. The only page naming a
country is `/offer`, and `/offer` carries no figures at all, so the two never meet.

## 2. Concept spine

**A local business with a profile and no website loses the customer at the exact moment he asks for
detail.** The page follows that one customer: he searches for the service, finds the profile on the map,
wants the detail, finds no website, and opens the competitor instead. Q8Block closes that gap by building
the website and then getting it found in Google search and in AI answers, and the page proves it with real
Search Console exports from sites it built.

**The word `rank` is out, 2026-09-24.** Ahmad: `I don't like the word ranking because this is outdated now.
We want something about discoverability with search engines and AI. They're like, what is ranking? What
does that mean?` So `rank`, `ranking`, `يتصدر`, `نتصدر` and `ترتيب` never carry the promise anywhere on the
site. The register is being found and being discovered, across Google search and AI assistants, set by the
approved hero headline `Customers find you on Google and in AI`. It survives only inside two factual FAQ
answers where it describes how search works. Section 3 is also reframed: the old headline told a reader with
no website that his website does not work, which describes somebody else's business. It now says the profile
already works and a website multiplies it.

The spine is brand led, not offer led. The headline is the outcome (customers find you); the offer is a
strip and a page, not the argument. `AI platform visibility` is one of the six real deliverables Ahmad
sells, so the NP Digital line Ahmad loves (`from Google to ChatGPT`) is honest here: it is a stated
deliverable, not a borrowed claim.

## 4. The numbers rule

Ahmad asked for this explicitly, 2026-09-24: an agent handed Search Console access sprinkles data through
the copy until it sounds stupid. Six rules, four from that day and two added after the board review the
same day, and they are enforced by the two registers at the end of `copy.md`.

1. **Figures appear in Section 6 and Section 7 only.** Nowhere else on the homepage and nowhere at all on
   `/offer`. Not in the problem section, not in what we do, not in what is included, not in the FAQ, not
   in the strip.
2. **No figure ever appears in a headline.** Headlines carry the idea; the figures sit in stage labels,
   metric lines and the table.
3. **A figure is never a promise or a forecast.** It is one named client, one named period, historical.
   `531 clicks` describes what kwtclean.com did in August 2026. It never describes what a new client
   will get.
4. **Everything comes from `design/proof-data.md` exactly as exported.** If it is not in that file it
   does not go on the page. Nothing is rounded, estimated, averaged across clients or taken from a
   partial month. September 2026 is partial and is excluded from every claim.
5. **One derivation is allowed, added 2026-09-24: the Section 7 growth percentage.** It is computed one
   way only, clicks in one named complete month against clicks in another named complete month, both
   exported in `proof-data.md`, rounded to a whole percent, and both months printed on the card so the
   reader can check the arithmetic. A site whose history cannot produce an honest growth figure gets the
   `مشروع جديد` / `New project` tag or no figure at all, never an invented one. Ahmad, 2026-09-24:
   `we don't want fake.` The full derivation, one row per card, is the last table in `copy.md`.
6. **Impressions are not printed on the page at all** after the 2026-09-24 revision. Section 6 prints
   clicks only, Section 7 prints percentages only. Impressions stay in `copy.md` as build data and as the
   check behind a percentage. A buyer with no website does not know what an impression is.

### How the proof is built, Section 6

**Rewritten 2026-09-24 to Ahmad's three stage narrative, and cut down.** His instruction was to
`avoid using stupid data`: the story leads, the figures support it. Month one is the build with essentially
no traffic, month two is the first discoverability with a very small amount of traffic, and month six is
the real result. Three figures on the page, clicks only: `7`, `165`, `531`.

**One client per curve, never a stitched best month.** The journey runs on kwtclean.com's own consecutive
months, April through August 2026. Picking the best month from a different site for each stage would look
stronger and would be dishonest, and a buyer who asks one follow up question would catch it.

**Month labels are stated precisely, not flattened.** April 2026 is kwtclean.com's first month with
recorded data, so August 2026 is its fifth full data month, close to six months of site age once the
indexing lag before the first click is counted. The stage label reads `About six months in, August 2026`
and the precision note printing exactly that arithmetic sits under the stages at the same size as the
claim. August is never labelled `month six` on its own.

**What was cut.** The printed five month table is gone, because a table of ten numbers is the wall of
numbers Ahmad objected to. The five rows stay in `copy.md` as build data, and the curve is still drawn from
all five so the line between month two and month six is continuous rather than a jump between two picked
months. The mashame3.com start note is also gone from this section: that site now has its own card and its
own real figure in Section 7, so the honest quiet start is still on the page without a third data point
crowding the story. Impressions are gone from every stage line.

**The baseline is kept, as two lines instead of a second band of figures.** kwtclean.com is the top of the
range partly because that client also worked the Google Business Profile checklist and built his reviews.
Most clients will not, so the section still states what the website alone holds: carwashkw.com between 199
and 440 clicks a month across 13 consecutive complete months. The copy never says `results may vary`. It
says: this is the top of the range when the client also does the profile work, and this is what the website
alone holds. Framed as a condition the client controls, so it doubles as an instruction. Required, not
optional (Ahmad, 2026-09-24).

### How the proof is built, Section 7

**Rebuilt 2026-09-24. All eleven SEO clients, not four.** Ahmad: show every one of them. The approved card
design is unchanged, and each card carries the site, the sector, one figure and a `view case study` link.

**Every figure is real and every figure is checkable.** Each card carries a click growth percentage computed
from two named complete months in `proof-data.md`, and both months are printed on the card. Six cards carry
a percentage. Four carry the `مشروع جديد` / `New project` tag because they have one complete month of data
and one month cannot produce growth: betikcleaner.com, anharpest.com, alghadeerclean.com, ragwaclean.com.
One card, movingcompanykw.com, is **held for Ahmad**: that site fell from 27 clicks in August 2025 to 11 in
August 2026, no honest window produces growth, and it is not new so the tag would be false. Three options
are written into `copy.md` Section 7, with dropping the card recommended. Until he picks, the grid is ten.

**Cards link out to the live client sites with `rel="nofollow noopener"` and `target="_blank"`** (Ahmad,
2026-09-24).

One caveat is flagged in `copy.md` for Ahmad: q8carwash.com's `+900%` is exact year on year arithmetic, 8
clicks to 80, but the site peaked at 193 in January 2026 and is below that now. A conservative swap is
written beside it, September 2025 to August 2026, `+281%`.

## 6. Palette and type (from DESIGN.md, not re-decided here)

**Palette.** Light section `#F5F6F7`. White `#FFFFFF`. Surface black `#141415` for the three dark bands.
Pure black `#000000` for the footer. Secondary dark `#26282C` for cards inside dark bands. Muted on dark
`#85898D`, muted on light `#6b6e72`, hairline on light `#EBEBEB`. One accent, orange `#FF5F29`, with
exactly three jobs: button fills, the highlight bar behind one or two headline words, small labels. It
never floods a surface, including the offer strip.

**The page is now light led with three dark bands.** Board C is a light hero, and the corrected structure
runs white and `#F5F6F7` from Section 1 to Section 5 and again at Section 8. The dark bands at Sections
2, 6 and 9 plus the black footer are the pattern breakers, which inverts the usual note in
`references/lessons.md` (one or two light breakers on a dark page) without breaking its logic: still a
small number of breakers, still never alternating. Each dark band is doing a job: the strip is chrome,
the journey is the proof, the final call is the close.

**Type.** Alexandria, variable, both scripts from one family. 600 for headings and labels, 400 for
buttons, 300 Latin body, 400 Arabic body. Arabic headings run at line height 1.25 desktop and 1.35
mobile, never NP's 1.1, and Arabic body is 400 not 300. Western numerals everywhere, every digit run
wrapped `dir="ltr"`. Latin eyebrow labels are uppercase with 0.06em tracking; Arabic eyebrow labels get
neither. Arabic type runs about 0.75x headings and 0.9x body against the English board values from the
body sections down (`lessons.md`), so the Arabic page is checked against the boards too, not only English.

**Radius vocabulary.** `0px` everywhere, `3px` on buttons, `100px` on pills. Nothing else.

**Shadows.** `none` at rest. Surfaces separate by background colour.

## 11. Build discipline

* The approved board is the spec, section by section: same images, type sizes, weights, line breaks and
  spacing. Writing the build from this brief instead of from the boards is the failure mode that got two
  previous client builds scrapped (`lessons.md`).
* **The board is the spec for the layout. `copy.md` is the spec for the words.** A board is a generated
  image and the model writes its own text into it. Two slips are already on record on this project: the
  hero board drew the wordmark as `Q8 digital`, and the Section 5 board drew a stock SEO list,
  `keyword research, content creation, link building, on page SEO, performance tracking, ongoing support`,
  which is not what Q8Block sells and is not in `copy.md`. The Section 5 list is the six deliverables in
  `copy.md`, item 1 of them `Custom website design and development`. Never read copy off a board.
* Board canvas is a 1440px screen. Measure the real type sizes off the board pixels and write them as
  `px/14.4` vw with clamps. Do not pick sizes by eye.
* Header and hero content sit in a centred 1500px column. Header and hero only.
* Write the locked spec into `clients/q8block/design/build-spec.md` before coding: fonts, the measured
  type scale, the sampled hex values, the section order, and where every image came from.
* Before showing Ahmad anything, run the board over build comparison per section in real Chrome at 1440
  and fix until they match, then sweep both locales at 1440x900 and 390x844 with broken=0, errors=0,
  overflow=false.
* Run `scripts/seo-audit.mjs` and clear every high finding before anything is pushed or goes live.
