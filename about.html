Our Story

Most Kuwait businesses are invisible on Google. We fix that.

Q8Block was founded on a single observation: businesses here have happy customers, solid reputations, and real value to offer — but when someone searches for what they do, they're nowhere to be found. A few competitors show up on Page 1 and capture all that intent. Everyone else gets nothing.

The difference isn't luck. It's SEO. And it's more achievable in Kuwait than most business owners realise, because the competition bar is still low compared to markets like Dubai or London. The window is open — but it won't stay that way.

We built Q8Block to help local businesses walk through it.
